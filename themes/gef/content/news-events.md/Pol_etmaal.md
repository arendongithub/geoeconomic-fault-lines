The Politicologenetmaal hosts annual political science workshops of the Low Countries (Belgium and the Netherlands). This year's edition will take place on the 11th and 12th of June 2026. The team will participate in the workshop titled _Geoeconomics and Economic Statecraft: Researching the Drivers, Consequences, and Institutional Implications._
